Find

Press ⌥V and type the name.

Take out

Drag it out as a file.

Drag a card to Finder, Figma, Mail — anywhere. The file leaves with the name you gave it. PDFs and other files are kept whole.

What Deck doesn't do

It doesn't record your clipboard.

Only what you choose with ⌥C is kept. Everything else you copy, passwords included, never reaches Deck.

It doesn't ask for permissions.

No Accessibility access, no screen recording, nothing to approve in System Settings.

It doesn't leave your Mac.

No account and no cloud. Your cards are stored on your own disk.

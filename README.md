# Deck

Give what you copy a name. A small shelf for your Mac: copy as usual, press ⌥C to keep it under a name, press ⌥V to find it by that name, and drag it out as a real file.

**[Download Deck for Mac](https://github.com/Jaycecode/deck-app/releases/latest/download/Deck.dmg)** — free, macOS 26.1 or later.

Website: https://jaycecode.github.io/deck-app/
